Does RYO's verdict change how often RYO's own plan works?

Reading the ledger…

Horizon

Settling next

Every deep_analysis answer carries a verdict and a bracket: a stop 1.5 ATR away and a target at +1R. These are today's, nearest to settling first. A red mark means the verdict leans against the direction of its own plan.

Settled

Which level OKX's hourly candles touched first. Neither means the window closed between the two; ambiguous means both fell inside one hour, which an hourly candle cannot order.

Does the verdict matter?

Target-first rate of one confluence state minus another, on the same days only, so the market's mood that day cancels out. The interval is resampled over days, not plans: plans locked on one day move together and are not independent.

Coverage

Every lock is a row, including the ones that failed. Nothing leaves this count quietly.

Method

  1. Lock. Once a day, deep_analysis is called for 25 majors and the whole answer is stored with its trace id: verdict, confluence state and score, the three gates, and the trade plan.
  2. Re-anchor. Settlement uses OKX, not RYO, so RYO does not grade itself. The stop and target keep their distance from RYO's entry in percent, applied to OKX's price at the moment of the lock. When the two prices differ by more than 2%, the lock is kept as basis mismatch and never settled.
  3. Settle. From the first full hour after the lock, OKX's closed hourly candles are walked forward until the stop or the target is touched, or the horizon ends.
  4. Compare. Rates are compared within days, and the 90% interval is a bootstrap over lock days with a fixed seed, so this page reads the same every time it loads.
